About

Design with strategy behind it.

As a Domestic Marketing Consultant specialising in graphic design, branding and digital media, I bring over five years of experience crafting visual identities and marketing campaigns for businesses, government ministries, NGOs and community organisations across Vanuatu.

My approach combines creativity with strategic thinking — every design is built to look right and work hard for the brand behind it. Recent work has expanded into live event social media management and short-form video animation, alongside the brand identity, campaign and print design this practice was built on.
